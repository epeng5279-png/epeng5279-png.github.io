About us

Neuroscience as a bridge between generations

We started as competitors. We became a community. Now we're building something that reaches far beyond the competition's stage: using learning as a common ground for people of every age.

Our mission

To harness the energy and dedication of young neuroscience enthusiasts like us, to create lasting impacts: nurturing the next generation of neuroscientists while pioneering cognitive health programs that bring the joy of learning to older adults.

Inspire

Plant the seeds of passion in learners young and old

Educate

Give all students the tools, knowledge, and mentorship to compete confidently at every level of the USA Brain Bee.

Connect

We believe the brain unites us, that our curiosity about it shouldn't fade with age. We're building a community that reflects that.

Our story

Born out of competition, driven by purpose

Aplysia Academy grew out of a simple realization: the students who compete in the USA Brain Bee come away with something rare — a genuine passion for neuroscience, a deep understanding of the brain, and the hunger to share it. We were those students.

After competing at the national level, we found ourselves asking the same question: what do we do with all of this? The answer was to build something. A tutoring program rooted in real experience. And beyond that, a grander vision for using the proceeds to develop neuroscience competitions for older adults: because competition can be exciting at any age.

Meet the people behind the initiative

We're a group of thirteen former USA Brain Bee competitors from across the country — united by a shared obsession with neuroscience and a belief that knowledge is most meaningful when it's shared. Each of us brings our experience to the tutoring room, and our passion for outreach to everything we build.
