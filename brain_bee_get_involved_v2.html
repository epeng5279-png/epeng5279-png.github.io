Tutors

Become a tutor

Are you a USA Brain Bee competitor with a passion for neuroscience and teaching? We'd love to have you. Tutors are the backbone of our program — sharing their expertise and competitive experience with the next generation of Brain Bee participants.

Deepen your knowledge

Teaching is one of the best ways to solidify your own understanding of neuroscience.

Flexible & remote

All sessions are conducted online — tutor from anywhere, on a schedule that works for you.

Earn compensation

Tutors receive a share of session revenue, with the remainder going toward our elder programs.

Join our community

Become part of a network of former competitors who share your love of neuroscience.

Ready to apply?

Our application takes about 5 minutes. We'll ask about your Brain Bee background, the subjects you're most confident teaching, and your availability.

Guest speakers

Share your expertise

Are you a neuroscientist, clinician, researcher, or educator with something valuable to share? Guest speakers bring real-world context to our curriculum and give students a window into what a life in neuroscience can look like.

We welcome speakers across a wide range of topics, including:

Neuroscience research & discovery
Clinical neurology & psychiatry
Cognitive health & aging
Neuroanatomy & imaging
Careers in neuroscience
Other — we're open to proposals

Interested in speaking?

Sessions are typically 30–60 minutes and held online. Get in touch and we'll follow up to discuss timing, format, and topic.
