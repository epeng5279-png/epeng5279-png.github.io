Built around BrainFacts

The majority of USA Brain Bee questions draw from BrainFacts: A Primer on the Brain and Nervous System. Our 17-session BrainFacts track works through the textbook chapter by chapter — so nothing gets left behind.

One-on-one tutoring

Personalized support

In addition to group sessions, students can sign up for individual tutoring with a national Brain Bee competitor. Choose how you'd like to be matched.

Regional match

Match with a regional competitor

Get paired with a Brain Bee competitor from your region — someone who knows your local competition, regional question patterns, and the specific circuit you're preparing for.

  • Tutor familiar with your regional Brain Bee
  • Targeted advice for local competition format
  • Subject to availability in your region
  • Closest thing to insider preparation
Open match

Match with any competitor

Get paired with a national Brain Bee participant regardless of region — great if regional tutors aren't available or if you want a broader range of perspectives on the material.

  • Wider pool of available tutors
  • Faster matching, more scheduling flexibility
  • Strong general Brain Bee expertise
  • Available to all students, always

Guest speakers

Insights from the frontier of neuroscience

Beyond curriculum sessions, we're building a speaker series that brings active researchers, clinicians, and neuroscience professionals directly to our students — sharing the discoveries, questions, and career paths that textbooks can't capture.

For students

Real research, real researchers

Guest speakers will give students a window into cutting-edge work happening right now — from neural circuits to neurological disease to brain-computer interfaces.

  • Live Q&A with working scientists and clinicians
  • Exposure to current research methodologies
  • Career insight from professionals across the field
  • Connection between competition prep and real science

Are you a researcher?

Share your work with the next generation

We're looking for neuroscientists, clinicians, and professionals who are passionate about science communication and mentorship. Sessions are informal, engaging, and fully remote — designed to fit around a busy research schedule.

  • Audience of motivated, competition-level students
