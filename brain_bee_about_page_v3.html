About us

Neuroscience as a bridge between generations

We started as competitors. We became a community. Now we're building something that reaches far beyond the competition stage — using the brain as common ground for people of every age.

Our mission

To harness the energy of young neuroscience competitors — and channel it into lasting impact: preparing the next generation of Brain Bee participants while pioneering cognitive health programs that bring the joy of learning to older adults.

Educate

We give students the tools, knowledge, and mentorship to compete confidently at every level of the USA Brain Bee.

Reinvest

Revenue from tutoring flows directly into developing free neuroscience programming for the elderly community.

Connect

We believe the brain unites us — and that curiosity about it shouldn't fade with age. We're building a community that reflects that.

Our story

Born out of competition, driven by purpose

The Brain Bee Initiative grew out of a simple realization: the students who compete in the USA Brain Bee come away with something rare — a genuine passion for neuroscience, a deep understanding of the brain, and the hunger to share it. We were those students.

After competing at the national level, we found ourselves asking the same question: what do we do with all of this? The answer was to build something. A tutoring program rooted in real competitive experience. And beyond that, a vision for using the proceeds to develop neuroscience competitions for older adults — because cognitive health matters at every stage of life, and because competition can be joyful at any age.

Our team

Meet the people behind the initiative

We're a group of thirteen former USA Brain Bee competitors from across the country — united by a shared obsession with neuroscience and a belief that knowledge is most meaningful when it's shared. Each of us brings our competitive experience to the tutoring room, and our passion for outreach to everything we build.
