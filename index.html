Founded by competitors, for the community

The Brain Bee Initiative was created by national Brain Bee participants who wanted to give back: Turning experience into tutoring, and tutoring into a greater mission to support cognitive health for all ages.

How it works

A self-sustaining cycle of learning

Every tutoring session does double duty, helping a student excel while generating the funding to serve a completely different community.

01

Neuroscience Tutoring

National Brain Bee participants offer expert tutoring for students preparing for the Brain Bee by covering neuroanatomy, the clinical setting, and more.

02

Reinvesting Revenue

Tutoring fees fund our operational costs and the development of elder programs.

Coming soon
03

Elder Competitions

As we grow, we'll build Brain Bee–style events for older adults. They will be free to enter, with monetary prizes to reward participation and growth.

Coming soon
04

A Growing Community

Students and elders will connect around a shared love of the brain: bringing generations together and fostering deeper bonds.

Our programs

Two communities, one mission

Beginning with youth tutoring, we are building towards a future where every age group can engage with neuroscience.

Youth

Brain Bee Preparation

Personalized tutoring from national Brain Bee competitors, with study resources for students at every level of the competition.

  • Online one-on-one and group tutoring sessions
  • Practice questions and mock exams
  • Mentorship from experienced competitors
  • Affordable pricing for all
Elders Coming soon

Competition for cognitive health

Our vision is to bring fun, social neuroscience competitions to older adults. Completely free to enter, with monetary prizes for top performers. This program is currently in development as we grow our reach and resources.
